MERP calculator

Modeled Emission Rate for Precursors — the Tier 1 PSD ozone/PM2.5 precursor screening arithmetic

What this tool is and isn't: this implements the three equations from EPA's Guidance on the Development of Modeled Emission Rates for Precursors (MERPs) (April 2019), as clarified by EPA's 30 April 2024 MERPs clarification memo. It does not supply an actual MERP or hypothetical-source value — those are derived from EPA's regional photochemical grid modeling of hundreds of hypothetical sources and are only available from EPA's MERPs VIEW Qlik application or a reviewing-authority-approved case-specific analysis. Reproducing those figures from memory here would risk citing a stale or wrong number in a permitting record, so this tool asks you to bring the MERP (or the hypothetical-source inputs behind it) and just does the downstream arithmetic and pass/fail comparison correctly.

Background

A MERP converts a source's precursor emissions (NOₓ, VOC, or SO₂, in tons/year) into an equivalent downwind secondary pollutant impact (ozone or PM2.5), so a permit applicant can screen a project against a Significant Impact Level (SIL) without running a full photochemical grid model. This is EPA's Tier 1 demonstration. If Tier 1 doesn't clear the project, EPA's Tier 2 allows a more sophisticated case-specific chemical transport model developed with the EPA Regional Office.

Eq.RelationshipUsed for
1MERP = SIL × Q_hyp ÷ ΔC_hyp Deriving a MERP (tons/year) from a hypothetical source's modeled emission rate (Q_hyp, tons/year) and its modeled air quality impact (ΔC_hyp, µg/m³ for PM2.5 or ppb for ozone)
2Project impact = Q_project × SIL ÷ MERP Estimating the project's own modeled impact once a MERP applies (algebraically equivalent to EPA's Q_project × (ΔC_hyp / Q_hyp))
3Projected design value = Project impact + Monitored design value A simple cumulative check against the NAAQS, using a representative monitor's design value as the background term

Sources & data notes

Equations 1–3: EPA, Guidance on the Development of Modeled Emission Rates for Precursors (MERPs), EPA-454/R-19-003, April 2019, equations 1 and 2 (this tool's Eq. 3 is that document's cumulative-impact walkthrough, not a separately numbered equation in the source).

Tier 1/Tier 2 framing and 2024 status: EPA, Guidance for Ozone and Fine Particulate Matter Permit Modeling (final, July 2022), and EPA's Clarification on the Development of Modeled Emission Rates for Precursors (MERPs) as a Tier 1 Demonstration Tool memo, 30 April 2024.

SIL values: mirrored from this site's own NAAQS page SIL table, not re-derived here — if that table is ever corrected, this page's dropdown needs the same fix applied by hand (it is a separate embedded copy, not a shared data source).

What this tool deliberately omits: EPA's actual illustrative/regional MERP values and the hypothetical-source library behind them. Those come from photochemical grid modeling EPA re-runs periodically and makes available only through MERPs VIEW; publishing a snapshot of them here would go stale silently. A passing Tier 1 screen here is a screening result, not a permit determination — confirm the hypothetical source selection and MERP value with your reviewing authority before relying on it.
