Who I am

I'm a Certified Consulting Meteorologist with over a decade of experience in air dispersion modeling, atmospheric science, and environmental data analysis. My work centers on the regulatory air quality modeling used for NAAQS compliance determinations and permitting — AERMOD, AERMET, AERSURFACE, and the surface and upper-air meteorological data that feed them — along with GIS analysis, remote sensing, and the statistical programming that ties it together.

I currently serve as a Senior Lead Consultant – Air Quality at Ramboll. I'm also a sitting Board Member of the Certified Consulting Meteorologist program with the American Meteorological Society.

I build tools. Regulatory meteorological data — NOAA's station inventories, period-of-record histories, the upper-air network that AERMET draws on — is public, but it's scattered across formats that were never designed to be looked at, let alone defended in front of a reviewer. This site is where I publish the tools I've built to make that data usable, along with the methodology behind them, so other people doing this work don't have to rebuild the same station parsers from scratch.

Outside of work

Most of what shows up in the tools on this site started as something I built for myself first. I run a home weather station and instrumentation setup, do amateur radio, and get out for astrophotography under reasonably dark New England skies. The same instinct that has me tuning a weather dashboard on a weekend is the one that had me build the station-inventory maps here on a weeknight because I was tired of NOAA's raw file formats.

This site, and everything on it, reflects my own independent views and work.
